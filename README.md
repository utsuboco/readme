# Utsubo

**Technology-first creative studio based in Osaka, Japan.**

We bring design and engineering together to create websites, web games, real-time 3D experiences, and interactive installations for brands in Japan and around the world.

[Website](https://www.utsubo.com/) · [Portfolio](https://works.utsubo.com/) · [Blog](https://www.utsubo.com/blog) · [Contact](https://www.utsubo.com/contact)

## What we do

- **Websites and web games:** brand and marketing sites, interactive storytelling, product visualization, and virtual showrooms.
- **Real-time 3D:** Three.js, WebGL, WebGPU, custom shaders, and performance optimization, alongside AR, VR, and XR experiences.
- **Interactive installations:** experiences that respond to movement, presence, or touch; projection mapping and generative visuals for exhibitions, museums, events, and public spaces.
- **Design:** branding, art direction, UI/UX, 3D modeling, animation, motion design, and sound design.
- **Technical direction and consulting:** prototyping, engineering, and integration, from concept through launch and on-site commissioning.

Learn more about our [studio](https://www.utsubo.com/about), [web and 3D development](https://www.utsubo.com/webgl-threejs-development), and [interactive installations](https://www.utsubo.com/osaka-interactive-installation-studio).

## Selected work

- [Waves of Connection — Expo 2025 Osaka](https://expo-2025.utsubo.com/): an interactive installation where visitors' movements bring waves to life.
- [Vectr](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/vectr-ai-startup-branding-case-study): brand identity and a WebGPU-powered website for an AI staffing platform.
- [Voyage to the Future](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oHh_iYVLttU): an interactive installation turning 1,800 messages into a shared story across three projection screens.

Explore more projects and experiments in our [portfolio](https://works.utsubo.com/).

## Open source

Our team builds and contributes to tools for the real-time graphics community. Projects include:

| Project | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [stats-gl](https://github.com/RenaudRohlinger/stats-gl) | FPS, CPU, and GPU performance monitoring for WebGL and WebGPU. |
| [r3f-perf](https://github.com/utsuboco/r3f-perf) | Performance monitoring for React Three Fiber applications using WebGL. |
| [react-three-next](https://github.com/pmndrs/react-three-next) | A starter for Next.js, React Three Fiber, and Three.js. |
| [Blender glTF tools](https://github.com/utsuboco/blender_gltf_scripts) | Blender utilities for glTF export, camera animation baking, and GPU instancing. |
